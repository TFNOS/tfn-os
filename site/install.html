Install TFN OS

This page finds your board, checks each file, and writes TFN OS to it through the USB port of your computer.

This browser cannot talk to a USB port. Open this page in Chrome or Edge on a computer (Windows, macOS, Linux or ChromeOS).

1Connect the board

Connect the board with a USB cable that carries data. Click Connect, then choose the port of the board in the list of the browser.

2Check your board

3Choose what to do

The newest release for this board is TFN OS .

INSTALL erases all data on the board: its settings, its WiFi, its apps and its files. The SD card does not change.
