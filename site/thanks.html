Thank you

TFN OS stands on the work of many people. Their licences ask for a notice, and the licences page and the NOTICE file of the source have it. This page is for something more: a thank you to each of these 46 projects and their authors.

The voice and the brain

They make a small board speak, listen and answer, with no internet.

  • tiny-voca

    n4ze3m; the ESP32-S3 port by ngxson

    The English voice that the board makes itself: it reads notifications, Wikipedia articles and books aloud.

  • dizione

    n4ze3m

    The phonemizer of tiny-voca: it turns English words into sounds. We ported it to C for the board.

  • CMUdict

    Carnegie Mellon University

    The pronunciation dictionary in the data of the phonemizer.

  • Oído

    Lokutor (Hashing Works)

    The speech-to-text engine of TALK: the board hears what you say.

  • Tiny-2.1

    WollyMonjac

    The small language model and its training kit: the brains of TALK, DETECTIVE and the quests.

The system

The base that TFN OS stands on.

  • ESP-IDF

    Espressif Systems

    The framework of the chips: the build, the drivers, WiFi, Bluetooth, the flash and the updates.

  • elf_loader

    Espressif Systems

    Loads each app file of the store into memory and links it to the system.

  • esptool-js

    Espressif Systems

    The install page of this site: it writes TFN OS to a board from the browser.

  • TinyUSB

    Ha Thach and the contributors

    The USB device stack: USB MIDI and the board as a USB microphone.

  • esp_wireguard

    Tomoyuki Sakurai, after Daniel Hope

    The WireGuard VPN of the board.

  • LovyanGFX and M5GFX

    lovyan03, M5Stack and the contributors

    The start tables of the panels of the Tab5 and the CoreS3.

  • M5Unified and the Bosch BMI270 API

    M5Stack; Bosch Sensortec

    The configuration file of the BMI270 motion sensor.

  • TweetNaCl

    D. J. Bernstein and others

    The X25519 key exchange of the TFN companions is written after it.

  • Box2D

    Erin Catto

    The method of tfphys, the small physics engine of the games.

  • The root certificates of Mozilla

    Mozilla, as curl publishes them

    The board checks HTTPS sites (the store, the weather) with them.

Pictures, sound and compression

The decoders that run on the board.

  • minimp3

    lieff

    Plays the MP3 music and the effects of the games.

  • TJpgDec

    ChaN

    Decodes JPEG pictures on the Tab5.

  • esp_new_jpeg

    Espressif Systems

    Decodes the frames of the DEMO video.

  • simplewebp

    Miku AuahDark, after libwebp of Google

    Decodes the WebP pictures of Wikipedia articles.

  • Zstandard

    Meta and the contributors

    Unpacks the offline Wikipedia files.

Knowledge and data

What the apps know about the world.

  • Wikipedia and Kiwix

    the Wikipedia authors; the Kiwix project

    WIKI reads Wikipedia from the SD card, from the offline files of Kiwix.

  • Open-Meteo

    Open-Meteo.com

    The weather of WEATHER and STANDBY, with no key and no account.

  • GeoNames

    GeoNames

    The city search of WEATHER.

  • ipwhois.io

    ipwhois.io

    Finds the place of the board for the first weather.

  • HYG Database

    David Nash (astronexus)

    The stars of SKY.

  • d3-celestial and Stellarium

    Olaf Frohn; the Stellarium project

    The constellation lines and names of SKY.

  • pyluach

    MS List

    The table of the weekly Torah readings of HEBREW CALENDAR.

  • Wikimedia Commons and Openverse

    many photographers

    The photos of WINE HELPER and RECIPES (each photo names its author on the board).
