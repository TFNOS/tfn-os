# The sound effects of MURANO

All of them have the CC0 licence (public domain). They come from the PC library of the project
(not in this tree: mono, 24000 Hz, 48 kbps), made from these packs. `mu_sfx.c` has them as C data (the
boards with no SD card play them too): `python libs/tfsfx/mk_sfx_c.py apps/murano/sfx apps/murano/mu_sfx MU
tink burst drop crack jacks whoosh coins`.

The furnace roar and the breath of the blow are not files: they are the two noise drones of the synthesizer
(`audio_drone`), so that they can go up and down with the glory hole and with the breath.
