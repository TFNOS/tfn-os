# The sound effects of GLASSHOUSE

All of them have the CC0 licence (public domain). They come from the PC library of the project
(not in this tree: mono, 24000 Hz, 48 kbps), made from these packs. `gl_sfx.c` has them as C data (the
boards with no SD card play them too): `python libs/tfsfx/mk_sfx_c.py apps/glasshouse/sfx apps/glasshouse/gl_sfx GL
crack shatter burst tinkle ting objbreak wood snap metal clang thud splash pour cut creak chain winch click select star`.
