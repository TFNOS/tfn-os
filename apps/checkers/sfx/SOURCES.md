# The sound effects of CHECKERS

All of them have the CC0 licence (public domain). They come from the PC library of the project
(not in this tree), made from these packs. `ck_sfx.c` has them as C data (the boards with no SD card play
them too): `python libs/tfsfx/mk_sfx_c.py apps/checkers/sfx apps/checkers/ck_sfx CK pick place hop take slide crown win lose bad`.

| File | What | Pack | Page |
|---|---|---|---|
| pick.mp3 | a piece is picked up (a tap or the start of a drag) | Kenney Casino Audio (chips-collide-4.ogg) | https://kenney.nl/assets/casino-audio |
| place.mp3 | a piece is put down on its square | Kenney Casino Audio (chip-lay-3.ogg) | https://kenney.nl/assets/casino-audio |
| hop.mp3 | a piece lands after a jump in a chain of captures | Kenney Impact Sounds (impactWood_light_002.ogg) | https://kenney.nl/assets/impact-sounds |
| take.mp3 | a taken piece pops off the board | OGA 3 Pop Sounds (3pops/pop1.ogg) | https://opengameart.org/content/3-pop-sounds |
| slide.mp3 | a piece slides under the finger (the drag) | Kenney Casino Audio (card-slide-6.ogg) | https://kenney.nl/assets/casino-audio |
| crown.mp3 | a man becomes a king | OGA Coin sounds (CoinSounds/Coin8.mp3) | https://opengameart.org/content/coin-sounds-1 |
| win.mp3 | the end of the game: a win | Kenney Music Jingles (jingles_PIZZI10.ogg) | https://kenney.nl/assets/music-jingles |
| lose.mp3 | the end of the game: a loss | Kenney Music Jingles (jingles_PIZZI14.ogg) | https://kenney.nl/assets/music-jingles |
| bad.mp3 | "no": a piece that cannot move, a drop on no legal square | Kenney Interface Sounds (error_004.ogg) | https://kenney.nl/assets/interface-sounds |
