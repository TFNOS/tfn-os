# The sound effects of THE TURNING WOOD

All of them have the CC0 licence (public domain). They come from the PC library of the project
(not in this tree: mono, 24000 Hz, 48 kbps), made from these packs. The footsteps come from the Kenney
pack with the same encoder settings (a tool that is not in this tree). `tw_sfx.c` has them as C data (the boards
with no SD card play them too) (a tool that is not in this tree makes it).

The owl, the bees and the bell of the end are tones of the synthesizer.
