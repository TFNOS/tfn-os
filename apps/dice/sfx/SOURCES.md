# The sound effects of DICE

All of them have the CC0 licence (public domain). They come from the PC library of the project
(not in this tree), made from these packs. `dz_sfx.c` has them as C data (the boards with no SD card play
them too): `python libs/tfsfx/mk_sfx_c.py apps/dice/sfx apps/dice/dz_sfx DZ shake throw die1 die2 add del tab win lose`.

| File | What | Pack | Page |
|---|---|---|---|
| shake.mp3 | the dice rattle while the board shakes | Kenney Casino Audio (dice-shake-2.ogg) | https://kenney.nl/assets/casino-audio |
| throw.mp3 | the first landing of a throw of two or more dice | Kenney Casino Audio (dice-throw-3.ogg) | https://kenney.nl/assets/casino-audio |
| die1.mp3 | a die hits the felt or another die | Kenney Casino Audio (die-throw-1.ogg) | https://kenney.nl/assets/casino-audio |
| die2.mp3 | a die hits the felt or the wall of the tray | Kenney Casino Audio (die-throw-4.ogg) | https://kenney.nl/assets/casino-audio |
| add.mp3 | a chip adds a die to the D&D set | Kenney Casino Audio (chip-lay-3.ogg) | https://kenney.nl/assets/casino-audio |
| del.mp3 | a hold removes a die from the set; CLEAR | Kenney Casino Audio (chips-stack-4.ogg) | https://kenney.nl/assets/casino-audio |
| tab.mp3 | a change of the mode | Kenney Interface Sounds (select_003.ogg) | https://kenney.nl/assets/interface-sounds |
| win.mp3 | a natural 20 | Kenney Music Jingles (Pizzicato jingles/jingles_PIZZI10.ogg) | https://kenney.nl/assets/music-jingles |
| lose.mp3 | a natural 1 | Kenney Music Jingles (Sax jingles/jingles_SAX01.ogg) | https://kenney.nl/assets/music-jingles |
