# The sound effects of BACKGAMMON

All of them have the CC0 licence (public domain). They come from the PC library of the project
(not in this tree), made from these packs. `bg_sfx.c` has them as C data (the boards with no SD card play
them too): `python libs/tfsfx/mk_sfx_c.py apps/backgammon/sfx apps/backgammon/bg_sfx BG shake throw1 throw2 bounce place stack slide hit off`.

| File | What | Pack | Page |
|---|---|---|---|
| shake.mp3 | the dice shake in the hand (a shake of the board, or a long roll) | Kenney Casino Audio (dice-shake-2.ogg) | https://kenney.nl/assets/casino-audio |
| throw1.mp3 | the dice fall on the board | Kenney Casino Audio (dice-throw-1.ogg) | https://kenney.nl/assets/casino-audio |
| throw2.mp3 | the dice fall on the board (a longer roll) | Kenney Casino Audio (dice-throw-3.ogg) | https://kenney.nl/assets/casino-audio |
| bounce.mp3 | a die hits the wall of the board | Kenney Casino Audio (die-throw-1.ogg) | https://kenney.nl/assets/casino-audio |
| place.mp3 | a checker is put down on a point | Kenney Casino Audio (chip-lay-3.ogg) | https://kenney.nl/assets/casino-audio |
| stack.mp3 | a checker is put down on other checkers | Kenney Casino Audio (chips-collide-4.ogg) | https://kenney.nl/assets/casino-audio |
| slide.mp3 | a checker slides on the board (the drag) | Kenney Casino Audio (card-slide-6.ogg) | https://kenney.nl/assets/casino-audio |
| hit.mp3 | a checker is hit and goes to the bar | OGA 80 CC0 RPG SFX (wood_03.ogg) | https://opengameart.org/content/80-cc0-rpg-sfx |
| off.mp3 | a checker goes off the board into the tray | Kenney Casino Audio (chips-stack-4.ogg) | https://kenney.nl/assets/casino-audio |
