# The sound effects of DIRT KINGS

All of them have the CC0 licence (public domain). They come from the PC library of the project
(not in this tree), made from these packs. `dk_sfx.c` has them as C data (the boards with no SD card play
them too): `python libs/tfsfx/mk_sfx_c.py apps/dirt_kings/sfx apps/dirt_kings/dk_sfx DK land tyre crash nitro coin
pickup lap mud win lose`.

| File | What | Pack | Page |
|---|---|---|---|

| tyre.mp3 | a tyre hits a hay bale or a wall (soft) | OGA 100 CC0 SFX (plop_02.ogg) | https://opengameart.org/content/100-cc0-sfx |
| crash.mp3 | a hard hit: a wall or another truck | OGA 75 CC0 breaking / falling / hit SFX (bfh1_metal_hit_03.ogg) | https://opengameart.org/content/75-cc0-breaking-falling-hit-sfx |
| nitro.mp3 | the nitro | OGA Boost or Launch or Thruster Sound Effect (boost_0.mp3) | https://opengameart.org/content/boost-or-launch-or-thruster-sound-effect |

| pickup.mp3 | a nitro can | Kenney Interface Sounds (confirmation_001.ogg) | https://kenney.nl/assets/interface-sounds |
